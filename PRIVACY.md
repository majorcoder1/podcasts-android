# Privacy policy — Podcasts

Podcasts is published by 4The People Media (Aaron McCreery, Crossville, Tennessee, USA).

- **No account, no analytics, no ads, no tracking.** The app does not collect, sell or share personal data. Your subscriptions, queue, downloads and listening progress are stored only on your phone.
- **Network use.** The app connects to podcast feed and audio servers you subscribe to or play, and to Apple's public iTunes search and charts service to let you discover shows. Those servers see your IP address and the request, as with any web request. No account or device identifier is sent by the app.
- **Notifications.** If you allow notifications, the app uses them on-device to tell you about new episodes.
- **Children.** The app does not collect data from anyone, including children.
